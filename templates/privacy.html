Privacy notice

{{ store.name }} collects the data below to run your order and to understand why people leave checkout without ordering.

What we record during checkout

  • When your checkout started, and your cart contents and value.
  • Your delivery distance and fee.
  • How long you spend on the payment step.
  • Back navigations, removed items and switching away from the page.

Location

Your exact delivery pin is stored only on orders you place, so we can deliver. If you leave without ordering, we keep only the distance in kilometres.

Payment

Card and e-wallet fields on this site are a demo and are never sent to our servers. Cash on delivery is the only real payment.

Accounts

You get an anonymous account when you first visit. Signing in with Google links your orders to your Google email.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see or delete your data. Contact the store to do so.
